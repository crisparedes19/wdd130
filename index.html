Tarija, Bolivia

Bolivia is a country in the heart of South America, home to the Andes mountains, the Amazon rainforest, and the Chaco plains. It has two capitals, Sucre and La Paz, and it's one of the most culturally rich countries in the region, with many Indigenous peoples, colorful festivals, and tasty food like salteñas. The photo you see is the Salar de Uyuni, the biggest salt flat in the world. When it rains, it turns into a giant mirror and you can't tell where the ground ends and the sky begins. Pretty cool, right?
